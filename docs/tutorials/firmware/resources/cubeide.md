# STM32CubeIDE

## What is STM32CubeIDE?

STM32CubeIDE is the primary IDE for microcontroller development.

## Download

Click [here](https://www.st.com/en/development-tools/stm32cubeide.html#st-get-software) to go to downloads page.

Download the version compatible with your device.

## Setup

{: .note}
This is setup for development. Come back here after completing the trainings.

By now you should have cloned the Avionics repo (Challenge 6). If not, go back and do that.

Open up STM32CubeIDE and go to **File >> Open Projects from File system...**.

<img width="622" height="647" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-03 at 11 30 44 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ad38d802-610d-4b7d-b251-116d32eee256" />

Look up the Avionics repo in **Directory**

<img width="883" height="627" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-03 at 11 35 03 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/071eb652-0e24-40d1-96e4-aaff890c1635" />

Typically, we only work on one or two projects at a time, so there isn't a need to import all the projects from the repo. You can uncheck the projects you don't need on the left hand side and then hit "Finish".
